Our story

The problem

In rural India, millions of women and girls face a daily reality shaped by silence, stigma, and exclusion. For generations, shame surrounding menstruation and barriers to economic opportunity have been passed from mother to daughter, eroding dignity and limiting possibility.

Our mission

We exist to cultivate dignity and unlock potential in rural India. Through integrated programs in health, livelihood, menstrual equity, and climate resilience, we empower women and children to build futures beyond their current circumstances.

We incubate, pilot, and scale community-led models across 8 Indian states and 4000+ villages.

Our approach

We place communities at the center of everything we do. We listen closely to understand real needs, co-create solutions with local partners, and pilot programs designed for relevance and sustainability.

Impact stories in motion

Restoring health through timely care: Kusum's anaemia recovery story

Kusum, a 32-year-old woman from Chainpura village in Niwai block of Tonk district, had been experiencing constant fatigue, weakness, and cramps, making even routine household work exhausting. During a health screening in March 2025, her haemoglobin level was found to be 6.5 gm/dl, indicating severe anaemia.
